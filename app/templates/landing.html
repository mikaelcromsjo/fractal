❄️Fractal Circle

Groups of any size discuss, vote, and narrow down to the best ideas — round by round, circle by circle — until a handful of proposals and representatives carry the whole group's voice forward.

Scales like a snowflake

The same small structure — a Circle of 4–8 people, discussing and voting — repeats at every level. 64 people become 8 Circles, whose representatives form 1 new Circle, and so on, until a single Circle holds the distilled result. No idea has to be read by everyone for everyone to be heard.

Works in Telegram or in any browser

Run it as a Telegram Mini App inside a private group chat, or send anyone a plain web link — fractal.ia-ai.se/app works standalone, no Telegram account required, so you can invite people either way.

Why "fractal"?

The structure repeats at every scale, like a snowflake's crystal branches: small circles discuss and vote, then hand their best ideas and a chosen representative up to the next, larger pattern — until the whole shape has been distilled to its essential form.

Round 1:  8 Circles of 8 people each (64 people)
           → Top 2 proposals per Circle = 16 proposals

Round 2:  8 Representatives (from 8 Circles) = 1 new Circle
           → Top 2 proposals from Round 2 = 2 proposals

Round 3:  1 Representative from Round 2
           → Fractal ends — final result
